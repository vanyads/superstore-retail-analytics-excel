# superstore-retail-analytics-excel
Where does a retail business make money, and where does it quietly lose it? This Excel project analyzes four years of Superstore orders (2014-2017) with PivotTables and slicers to trace sales and profit, find the discount level where orders turn unprofitable, and pinpoint the products, segments, and regions that drive or drain profit.

# Project Overview
The goal of this project is to understand where the business makes and loses money and to answer practical questions such as:
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- At what discount level do orders start to lose money?
- Which categories, sub-categories, products, segments, regions, and states drive (or hurt) profit?

# Dataset
- Size: about 10,000 order lines (rows)
- Key fields: Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode, Customer Name, Segment, State, Region, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name, Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit

# Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and Calculated Fields
- PivotCharts and Slicers
- Dashboard design
